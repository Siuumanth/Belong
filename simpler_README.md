# Belong — Relationships Over Swiping

Modern dating apps are built largely around attraction, shared interests, and surface-level preferences. Belong takes a different approach by focusing on deeper relationship compatibility—understanding what people need, value, expect, and bring to a relationship, then using AI to identify meaningful connections beyond simple similarity.

---

## 💡 How Belong Works

Belong replaces superficial swiping with a 2-stage matching system:

```
  1. Chat & Learn (AI Onboarding)
            │
            ▼
  2. Smart Screening (Location, Age & Gender)
            │
            ▼
  3. Two-Way Compatibility Match (Forward & Backward)
            │
            ▼
  4. Deep Relationship Analysis (4 Core Dimensions)
```

---

### 1. Conversational Onboarding
Instead of filling out static forms, users have a natural chat with an AI guide. The system understands two main parts of your dating profile:
- **`self`**: Who you are, your lifestyle, values, conflict style, and what you bring to a relationship.
- **`wants`**: What you are explicitly looking for in a partner.

---

### 2. Smart Screening
Before doing any deep matching, Belong instantly screens out non-matches using basic dealbreakers:
- **Distance**: Only connects people living near each other.
- **Age**: Respects both users' preferred age ranges.
- **Gender**: Ensures mutual gender preferences match on both sides.

---

### 3. Two-Way Compatibility Matching
Matching in Belong is never one-sided. The system checks compatibility in both directions:
- **Forward Match**: Does this person fit what **you** are looking for?
- **Backward Match**: Do you fit what **this person** is looking for?

A candidate is only recommended if **both people satisfy what the other is seeking**.

---

### 4. Deep Relationship Analysis (4 Core Dimensions)
Candidates who pass two-way matching are evaluated across **4 core compatibility dimensions**:

1. **Emotional Needs**: How each person handles stress and what kind of support or reassurance they need.
2. **Core Values**: Shared principles around family, ambition, honesty, and growth.
3. **Lifestyle & Routine**: Daily habits, schedules, and active vs. relaxed routines.
4. **Conflict & Communication Style**: How each person navigates disagreements and handles space vs. discussion.

---

## 🎯 Summary

Belong focuses on **compatibility that lasts** — connecting people who naturally complement each other's lives, values, and emotional needs.
